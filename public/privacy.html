Privacy Policy for FocusFlow

Last Updated: January 14, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in FocusFlow (the "App").

1. Data Collection and Usage

We believe in privacy by default. FocusFlow does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) on external servers.

All data generated by the App, including your tasks, timer settings, and preferences, is stored strictly locally on your device using standard web storage technologies (LocalStorage/IndexedDB). We do not have access to this data.

2. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics services, tracking tools, or advertising networks.

3. Children’s Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any user data, we are compliant with requirements regarding children's privacy.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. New changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
